## What and Why?

**Jargonot** is a mobile web app designed to make news articles and research papers more accessible to high school and undergraduate students. Many students are curious about topics like AI, finance, science, and policy but are discouraged by technical jargon and complex structures.

Jargonot bridges this gap by:

- Simplifying jargon into accessible language.
- Explaining key terms and concepts.
- Providing background information and context.
- Highlighting why developments matter.
- Offering different levels of depth for exploration.
- Linking back to original sources.

Our goal is to empower students to gradually build the knowledge needed to engage with advanced materials directly.

---

## For Whom?

Jargonot is for **students curious about topics outside their expertise**. Examples include:

- A computer science student exploring finance.
- A high schooler interested in AI.
- An engineering student following international policy.

We are part of the target audience, allowing us to test and refine the app with real users.

---

## How?

Jargonot will present news and research in an approachable format:

1. **What happened?** – A clear summary of the event.
2. **Why does it matter?** – Its significance and impact.
3. **What should I know?** – Key terms and background concepts.
4. **Go deeper** – Detailed explanations for curious readers.
5. **Original source** – Links to the full article or paper.

The app will adapt to users' knowledge over time, focusing on unfamiliar concepts and providing personalized learning paths.

---

## Scope

Jargonot is achievable within one semester for a team of 4–6 programmers. The initial version will include:

- User accounts and topic selection.
- A feed of simplified articles and research papers.
- Explanations of jargon, terms, and context.
- Saving articles and tracking learned concepts.

Advanced features like personalized knowledge profiles and recommendation algorithms can be added later.

---

## Vision

We aim to transform curiosity into understanding by making complex information approachable. Jargonot will help students move from:

> "This looks interesting, but I don't understand it."

to:

> "I understand what happened, why it matters, and I know enough to learn more."
